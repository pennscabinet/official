FAQ, Shipping & Returns

Everything you need to know about ordering from Penn's Cabinet — how things ship, how returns work, and the questions we get asked most often.

Shipping Policy

How Things Get to You

We ship Monday through Friday from a small studio in the U.S. Most orders go out within one business day of purchase.

How fast do orders ship?

Orders placed before 2pm local time on a business day typically ship the same day. Anything after that, or on weekends and holidays, ships the next business day.

Once it's in the mail, you'll get a tracking number by email — usually within an hour of the label being printed.

What carriers do you use?

USPS for most domestic orders (Ground Advantage, Priority Mail, or First-Class for small items). UPS for higher-value or oversized parcels. Tracking is included on every order.

How much does shipping cost?

Shipping is calculated at checkout based on weight, destination, and chosen service. We don't pad shipping — you pay actual carrier rates.

Free shipping automatically applies on U.S. orders over $50.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — we ship to most countries via USPS International or UPS Worldwide. International rates are calculated at checkout. Please note that any customs duties, taxes, or import fees are the buyer's responsibility.

How do you pack high-value items?

Cards ship in penny sleeves + top-loaders + team bags inside a rigid mailer. Graded slabs are wrapped, padded, and double-boxed when needed. Comics ship between two extra-thick boards in a Gemini Mailer or equivalent. Figures ship in cushioned boxes — never naked in a poly mailer.

Orders over $200 automatically include signature confirmation.

My package is stuck in transit / says delivered but I don't have it.

First, check around your delivery area, with neighbors, and at your local post office — carriers occasionally mark items delivered a day early. If 48 hours pass with no sign of it, email us at penns.cabinet@gmail.com with your order number and we'll open a carrier investigation together.

Returns & Refunds

If Something's Off, We'll Make It Right

We want every package to land well. If it doesn't, here's how we sort it out — quickly and without a fight.

What's your return window?

You have 14 days from the delivery date to request a return on most items. Items must be returned in the same condition you received them — unsealed sealed product, unsleeved cards, or damaged packaging may affect your refund.

What can't be returned?

A small set of items are final sale:

  • Opened sealed product (boxes, packs, blisters)
  • Graded items where the slab has been cracked or tampered with
  • Custom or made-to-order items
  • Items marked "Final Sale" on the product page

If a final-sale item arrives damaged or not as described, the damage policy below still applies — reach out.

My item arrived damaged or not as described.

Email penns.cabinet@gmail.com within 72 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos of the item and packaging. We'll either send a replacement (when possible) or issue a full refund including return shipping. Damage claims aren't a hassle here — we'd rather make it right.

How do I start a return?

Email us with your order number and a quick note about why you're returning the item. We'll send back a return label and instructions. Once we receive and inspect the item, your refund posts to the original payment method within 3–5 business days.

Who pays for return shipping?

If we made a mistake — wrong item, damaged item, item not as described — we cover return shipping, full stop. For change-of-mind returns, the buyer covers return postage. Either way, we'll provide a prepaid label so you don't have to deal with the post-office line.

Can I exchange instead of return?

For most items, yes. Email us with the order number and what you'd like to swap to, and we'll check stock and let you know the difference (or store credit) right away.

General FAQ

Other Things People Ask

Quick answers to the questions that come up most often. If you don't see yours, drop us a line and we'll add it here.

Are your items authentic?

Yes. Every item is hand-inspected before listing. Sealed product comes from trusted distributors and original collections. Higher-value singles, comics, and figures are sourced from collections we know personally or from established dealers, and we list condition notes plainly on each product page.

If anything ever turns out to be inauthentic, we'll refund you in full and cover return shipping — no questions.

Do you grade cards or comics in-house?

We don't grade in-house. Items listed as graded come pre-slabbed by PSA, BGS, CGC, or another recognized service, and we list the cert number on the product page. Raw items are described with our own condition notes plus high-resolution photos so you can judge for yourself.

Can I put a hold on an item?

For items over $100, yes — we can hold for up to 48 hours with a small deposit. Email us with the product link and we'll get it set up.

Do you buy collections?

Sometimes! We focus on quality over quantity. If you've got a collection of cards, comics, vintage figures, or retro games you're considering selling, send a few photos and a list to penns.cabinet@gmail.com and we'll let you know if it's a fit.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes — free of charge during the holidays, and a $4 add-on the rest of the year. Add a note at checkout with the message you'd like included and we'll handwrite it on a card.

How can I get in touch?

The fastest way is email at penns.cabinet@gmail.com. We also read every DM on Instagram. More info on our About page.

Privacy Policy

Your Data, Plainly Explained

We collect what we need to get you your order and not much else. Here's exactly what that looks like.

What information do you collect?

When you place an order or sign up for our newsletter, we collect:

  • Name, shipping address, and email
  • Phone number (only if you provide it for shipping notifications)
  • Order history with us
  • Basic technical info — IP address, browser type, pages visited — collected automatically by our hosting and analytics tools

Payment details (card numbers, etc.) are handled directly by our payment processor and never touch our servers.

How do you use my information?

To fulfill your order, communicate about it, and — if you've opted in — send occasional updates about new arrivals. We also use aggregate data to understand which corners of the shop people enjoy, so we can stock more of the good stuff.

We do not sell your information. Ever.

Who do you share information with?

Only the partners we need to run the shop:

  • Shopify — our storefront and payment platform
  • Shipping carriers (USPS, UPS) — to deliver your order
  • Email service — for order confirmations and (if subscribed) the newsletter

Each of these has their own privacy policies which we recommend reviewing if you'd like the full picture.

What about cookies?

We use cookies for things like keeping your cart contents, remembering preferences, and basic analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some shop features may not work as smoothly without them.

What rights do I have over my data?

You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (subject to any legal obligations to retain order records). Email penns.cabinet@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy Request" and we'll respond within 30 days.

If you're in the EU, UK, or California, you have additional rights under GDPR/CCPA — those apply here too.

How long do you keep my data?

Order records are kept for as long as required for tax and accounting purposes (typically 7 years). Newsletter subscription data is kept until you unsubscribe. Anything else is removed when it's no longer needed.
