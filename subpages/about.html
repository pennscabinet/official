Our Story

Collectors Serving Collectors

Penn's Cabinet is a small, queer-owned shop built around one simple idea: the things we collect deserve to be treated with care — by the person selling them, and the person buying them.

A cabinet of curiosities, carefully kept & honestly priced.

Penn's Cabinet started where most great collections start — in a single overflowing closet. What began as a personal stash of trading cards, comics, and oddball figurines slowly turned into shelves, then bins, then a full-on operation. We finally decided that the most fun thing to do with a hoard like this is share it.

We hand-pick everything in the shop. Cards are inspected, sleeved, and priced against current comps. Comics are bagged and boarded. Figures are checked for completeness — accessories, paperwork, the works. If a piece has flaws, we'll tell you. If it's a sealed grail, we'll celebrate it with you.

What we believe: good collectibles outlast trends, condition matters, and the hobby gets better when more people are welcome at the table.
